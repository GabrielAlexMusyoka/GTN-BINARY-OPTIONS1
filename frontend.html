How to Trade

Your complete GTN Binary Options guide

1. The Basics

GTN Binary Options is a fast-execution prediction platform. You place a stake (minimum $5.00) on a short price or digit outcome (1–5 seconds). If your prediction is right, you receive your stake back plus a fixed 80% profit payout. If wrong, you lose only the stake — never more.

2. Demo vs Real

Switch any time using the account pill at the top:

  • Demo — practice with $1,000.00 virtual funds. No real money at risk.
  • Real — uses your deposited balance ($5.00 min deposit). Wins/losses are real.

3. Markets — Volatility Indices

We offer synthetic Volatility Indices (Vol 10 (1s), Vol 25, Vol 50, Vol 75, Vol 100). They run 24/7 and aren't affected by news or market hours — pure price action.

4. Contract Types — Deep Dive

Rise / Fall 80% Profit

Predict whether the next tick price finishes higher (RISE) or lower (FALL) than your entry price.

Example: Entry 9450.34. Stake $10 on RISE. Next tick 9450.61 → higher → you win $8.00 profit ($18.00 returned).
💡 Best on calmer indices (Vol 10, Vol 25) where directional drifts are steady.
Even / Odd 80% Profit

Predict whether the LAST decimal digit (0–9) of the next tick will be even (0,2,4,6,8) or odd (1,3,5,7,9).

Example: Current tick 9450.32 (digit 2 → even). Stake $5 on ODD. Next tick 9450.47 → digit 7 → odd → you win $4.00 profit ($9.00 returned).
💡 Use the 0–9 frequency strip to spot short-term digit imbalances.
Over / Under 80% Profit

Pick a barrier digit 0–9, then predict whether the last digit of the next tick will be OVER (greater than) or UNDER (less than) the barrier.

Example: Set barrier = 4, choose OVER. You win if the next digit is 5, 6, 7, 8, or 9.
💡 Barrier 4 for Over and Barrier 5 for Under offer balanced 5-digit coverage.
Matches / Differs 80% Profit

Pick one digit 0–9. MATCHES wins if the next tick's last digit equals your pick; DIFFERS wins if it is any of the other 9 digits.

Example: Pick digit 7, tap DIFFERS with $10. Next tick ends in 3 → win $8.00 profit ($18.00 returned).
💡 Combine with the AI Entry Scanner to target cold digits.

5. Placing a Trade

  1. Choose a synthetic market (e.g., Volatility 10 (1s)).
  2. Pick a contract type (Rise/Fall, Even/Odd, Over/Under, Matches/Differs).
  3. Enter your stake ($5.00 minimum).
  4. Tap the green or red button for your prediction.
  5. Result settles on the next ticks — your wallet updates automatically.

6. Auto-Trade

Switch to AUTO to run a strategy hands-free:

  • Target Profit: $200 default — bot stops automatically when reached.
  • Stop Loss: $999 default — maximum loss safety stop.
  • Multiplier: 2x default — stake multiplies after a loss (Martingale recovery).

7. Deposits & Withdrawals

• MPESA / Crypto Deposits: Minimum $5.00 instant funding.
• Withdrawals: Minimum $10.00 profit payout processed to your registered channel.

8. Risk Management

  • Never stake more than you can afford to lose.
  • Start small on Real — use Demo ($1,000) to test strategies first.
  • Set tight Auto-Trade Stop Loss limits to protect your capital.
